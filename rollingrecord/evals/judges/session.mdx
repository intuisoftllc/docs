Session-aware judges read the `_session` trace to assert on which tools were called, in what order, and within what round budget.

## What this covers

- `MustCallToolJudge`, `MustNotCallToolJudge`, `RoundBudgetJudge`, `ToolCallSequenceJudge` (`evals/judges/session_judges.py`) reading `_session.all_tool_calls`/`terminal_reason`.
- The `expected` keys they consume (`must_call_tools`, `must_not_call_tools`, `round_budget`, `tool_call_sequence`) and their opt-in SKIP behavior.
- Why the multi-round trace (not just the final answer) is what these grade.

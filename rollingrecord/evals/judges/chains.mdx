Chain primitives compose individual judges into a decision pipeline, ordering cheap deterministic checks before expensive LLM grading.

## What this covers

- `Cascade`, `AllOf`, `AnyOf`, `Majority`, `MinConfidence` composition semantics and the PASS/FAIL/UNCERTAIN/SKIP truth table (`evals/judges/chains.py`).
- Cost = sum of children that ran; cheap-deterministic-first, expensive-LLM-last ordering; `MinConfidence` as the escalation knob.
- How child verdicts nest into `details.children` (rendered by the runner's verdict tree).

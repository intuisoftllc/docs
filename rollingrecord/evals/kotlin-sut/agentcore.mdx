`agentcore` holds the `AgentLoop` extracted verbatim from the app, so evals drive the exact production loop.

## What this covers

- `AgentLoop.kt` (`app/agentcore`): the KB-chat tool loop extracted verbatim from `KnowledgeBaseAdvisor`, driving both the Android app and the eval runner; round budget from `ServerConfig.kbChatMaxToolRounds` + fallback final round.
- `AgentTurnRequest`/`AgentTurnOutcome`, the full `AiEngineResult`→user-string mapping, `hadWriteOp`, and response sanitization.
- What the loop does NOT own (pre-flight, tool dispatch behind `ToolExecutor`, analytics) plus wire models under `data/remote/aiengine/model`.

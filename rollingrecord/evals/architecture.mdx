An eval case flows from `run.py` through the runner and a system-under-test into the judge chain, producing a verdict, results JSON, and analytics.

## What this covers

- End-to-end path: `run.py` → `Runner` → `SystemUnderTest`/`SessionRunner` → judge chain → `Verdict` → results JSON + `l_eval_run` analytics.
- The three SUT backends behind one `SystemUnderTest` ABC (`evals/core/system.py`): `AiEngineHttp` (Python), `KotlinSessionDriver` (`--sut=kotlin`), and on-device `OnDeviceSystemUnderTest`.
- Where fidelity is deliberately imperfect vs production (documented in `evals/core/session.py` and `evals/ai_engine/kotlin_runner.py` headers).

`SuiteRegistry` is the Kotlin analog of each suite's `judge_for_case`, wiring the same structural + LLM judge fan-out on-device.

## What this covers

- `suites/SuiteRegistry.kt` = the Kotlin analog of every `judges.py` `judge_for_case`, wiring all 14 suites with the standard structural-`AllOf` + `Cascade(MinConfidence(LlmJudge Sonnet), LlmJudge Opus)` fan-out.
- The ported judge families under `judges/` (Deterministic, Fuzzy, Semantic, Llm, Faithfulness, GroundedExtraction, Retrieval, SessionJudges, Chains) and `Anthropic.kt` for direct judge calls.
- `data/CaseLoader.kt` loading bundled `assets/evals` case JSON.

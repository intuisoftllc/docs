The exit-code policy combines the pass floor, regression detection, and flake accounting to decide whether a run succeeds.

## What this covers

- Exit-code policy in `run.py` `_print_summary`: 0 (rate ≥ floor, no regressions), 1 (below floor OR any regression), 2 (run aborted/failed); default floor `0.95` in `config.py`.
- Flake accounting (`is_flake` excluded from denominator + exit code) and `--repeat` reliability badges (reliable ≥95% / flaky 50–94% / broken <50%).
- Regression always breaks the build regardless of floor.

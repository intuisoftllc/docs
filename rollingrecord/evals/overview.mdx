The eval harness evaluates the AI behavior of RollingRecord's KB-chat bot and related operations, and runs in two places that share the same case format.

## What this covers

- What the harness evaluates: AI behavior of the KB-chat bot (`kb_query`) plus `template_moderation`/`extract`; graded model is `claude-haiku-4-5` (`evals/config.py` `PRODUCTION_MODEL`), judged by Sonnet/Opus.
- The two runtimes that share the same case format: the Python harness in `evals/` and the on-device Kotlin port in `app/app/src/debug/.../evalmode/`.
- The Python→Kotlin parity direction: `--sut=kotlin` drives the LITERAL production loop via `app/agentrunner` + `app/agentcore`.

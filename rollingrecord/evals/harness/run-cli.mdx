`run.py` is the entry point for the Python harness, with flags for scoping, dry-runs, and choosing the system-under-test.

## What this covers

- Every flag from `evals/run.py` `_parse_args`: `--all`, `--suite`, `--bucket`, `--case`, `--dry-run`, `--scope`, `--show-pass`, `--pass-floor`, `--build-runner`, `--sut={python,kotlin}`, `--repeat N`.
- Mutually-exclusive scope group (`--all` vs `--suite`), `--case` cross-suite discovery, and `--build-runner` build-only shortcut (forces `--sut=kotlin`).
- Summary printing + exit-code policy (`_print_summary`) and repeat-mode reliability badges (`_print_repeat_summary`: reliable/flaky/broken).

## What this covers

- Three fixture forms in `evals/core/mock_tools.py`: loose, explicit `{match,result}` list, and staged progressive-disclosure; match operators (exact / `_contains` / `_in`).
- `READ_ONLY_TOOL_DEFAULTS` safe-empty fallbacks vs `MockNotFound` for unmocked write tools; `MockResolution` trace records.
- Stage advancement keyed to user turns (sticky-last clamp), driven by `advance_stage()`.

Extraction is fully asynchronous across three functions, with all job state stored as encrypted columns rather than a Storage bucket.

## What this covers

- Three functions: extract-submit (client submit + reserve-only headroom check), extract-runner (server-paced tick), extract-results (fetch/ack/status/cancel); single-flight enforced by a partial unique index (extraction_already_running).
- Payload/accumulator/results live as encrypted columns on extraction_jobs (payload_ct, state_ct, results_ct under one per-job DEK in dek_ct) — the former job-plane Storage bucket was removed (RR-503).

extract-runner claims one job, processes chunks within a wall-clock budget, checkpoints after each, and crash-resumes from the last durable checkpoint.

## What this covers

- WebhookSecret (EXTRACT_RUNNER_SECRET); atomically CLAIMs one job, processes chunks within a ~100s wall-budget, checkpoints after every chunk, yields/re-kicks or finalizes; crash-resumes from the last durable checkpoint.
- Imports ExtractHandler from _shared/extraction/extract-handler.ts directly (no network hop); per-recording reserve-on-chunk-0 / commit-on-last-chunk metering via extraction_run_id idempotency.
- Two-lane errors: retryable (throw, heartbeat sweeper requeues) vs poison (extraction_undecryptable, fail immediately) — runner-classify.ts.

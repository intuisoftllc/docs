sync-reserve mints and wraps a per-file key and signs an upload URL; sync-audio handles encrypted download and sanctioned deletes.

## What this covers

- sync-reserve: a stateless signed-URL issuer — mints a DEK, wraps it under the KEK, signs an upload URL at audio-storage/&#123;uid&#125;/&#123;rid&#125;.enc, writes zero rows; backup_quota_exceeded.
- sync-audio: download (unwrap DEK + signed GET) and delete (the sanctioned orphaned-blob removal on rolling demote); grace-window download throttling via sync_download_grace.

sync-push applies batched upserts and tombstones, returning a per-operation outcome array rather than failing the whole batch.

## What this covers

- Batched upserts + tombstones with a per-op outcome array (accepted/server_wins/rejected); top-level 400 only for structural abuse; tombstones reordered before upserts.
- Per-op codes: sync_disabled_for_group, blob_missing, size_mismatch (over 1% goes to l_user_abuse_event), batch_too_large, fk_violation; a sanctioned c_recordings DELETE (tombstone) path.

Cross-device sync replicates a set of client-encrypted c_* tables under a last-writer-wins model across a small set of endpoints.

## What this covers

- c_* replicated tables (c_recordings, c_transcripts, c_contexts, c_context_cards, c_search_messages, c_settings, junctions); client-side encryption (ciphertext columns) with a per-user DEK; LWW conflict model.
- Endpoints: sync-reserve to sync-push to sync-pull to sync-reconcile, plus sync-audio, sync-group-exclude, user-sync-preferences.

Transcription is metered in measured milliseconds against minute-based tier caps, and cost is recorded to the financials table.

## What this covers

- transcription_ms counter (actual measured ms, not a count); tier caps in minutes converted to ms at read time; cost (base + diarize + medical add-ons) to f_user_financials.ai_cost_micros via incrementFinancialColumn; h_transcribe_history.

Three inbound webhooks drive identity, entitlements, and transcription completion: clerk-webhook, revenuecat-webhook, and assemblyai-webhook.

## What this covers

- clerk-webhook: Svix-signed; user.created provisions the row; user.deleted triggers a full GDPR wipeUser().
- revenuecat-webhook: shared-secret only (App Check + App Version opted out); authoritative writer of lifetime_entitlements; mirrors only WEBHOOK_MIRRORED_LOOKUP_KEYS; handles bundle credit grants/refunds and records transcription/LLM cost.
- assemblyai-webhook: completion callback writes transcribe_results, commits metered ms, records cost to f_user_financials, records h_transcribe_history.

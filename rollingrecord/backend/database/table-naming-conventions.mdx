Table-name prefixes encode purpose and retention — long-retention logs, short-retention history, financial state, and cloud-synced tables.

## What this covers

- l_* long-retention audit/compliance/financial-event logs (l_user_key_access, l_admin_audit, l_user_abuse_event, l_deleted_identity, l_credit_consumption, l_entitlement_events), swept by prune_log_tables().
- h_* short-retention support history (h_transcribe_history, h_ai_engine_sessions), swept by prune_h_tables(); f_* financial state (f_user_financials); c_* cloud-synced tables.

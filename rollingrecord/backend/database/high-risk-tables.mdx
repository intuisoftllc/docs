A small registry marks the tables whose loss is unrecoverable, with strict protections around them.

## What this covers

- Criteria (no engineering recovery path); registry entries c_recordings (crypto columns dek_ct/audio_iv/dek_iv/kek_version/storage_path) and user_crypto (per-user DEK).
- Protections: no raw DELETE outside sanctioned callers, no TRUNCATE/DROP, PITR + daily off-platform backup, plus watched-but-not-high-risk tables (canonical_users, lifetime_entitlements).

The first three gates reject a request before any auth work — wrong method, missing App Check, or an under-floor app version.

## What this covers

- method.ts (POST/GET/PATCH allowlist), app-check.ts (verifies X-Firebase-AppCheck with Firebase), app-version.ts (compares X-App-Version against min_supported_app_version_code).
- The App Version floor emits client_too_old (410) with a Play Store deep-link; bootstrap/server-to-server callers opt out.

Developer bypass is a quota exception, not a validation-or-cost exception — structural checks and cost accounting always run.

## What this covers

- What dev bypass MAY skip: Gate 4 entitlement, Gate 5 caps + enforced quota-counter increments, Gate 6 rate limits.
- What it MUST NEVER skip: structural validation (App Check, body parse, operation/model allowlist, transcribe duration format, kb_query round budget) AND cost accounting (f_user_financials.ai_cost_micros) + token-throughput analytics (usage.input_tokens/output_tokens).
- The classification rule for new checks: a limit/quota is dev-skippable; structural/format/safety is unconditional.

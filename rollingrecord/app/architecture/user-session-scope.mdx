Every account runs in its own session scope, rebuilt on each sign-in and sign-out so no state leaks between users.

## What this covers

- `UserSessionManager.switchTo()` tears down and rebuilds `UserSessionScope` on every sign-in/out; `UserSessionModule` wiring.
- The `UserScopedCache` + `userScopedCacheRegistry` pattern and owner-stamping to prevent cross-account leakage (`UsageRepository` reference impl).
- `SessionDekHolder`, `UserScopedInject`, and `UserWorkTag` for session-scoped work tagging.

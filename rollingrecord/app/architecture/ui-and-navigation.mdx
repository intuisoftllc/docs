The app's UI is built entirely in Jetpack Compose, rooted at `V2App.kt`.

## What this covers

- The Compose UI surface (`ui/v2`, rooted at `V2App.kt`) and how the app composes its screens.
- Navigation: `V2MainNavHost` / `V2MainRoutes` for the signed-in app, and `V2LoginNavHost` / `V2LoginRoutes` for the pre-sign-in flow.
- How the root hosts app-wide surfaces (the App Lock overlay, onboarding, and recovery dialogs).

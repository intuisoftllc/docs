Cloud audio is prefetched to warm the local cache and fetched on demand when needed.

## What this covers

- `CloudPrefetchWorker` / `CloudPrefetchScheduler` warming the local cache; `CloudDownloadManager` on-demand fetch.
- `CloudDecryptFailureStore` for undecryptable-blob tracking.

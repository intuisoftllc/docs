Recording is backed by an encoder abstraction that supports AAC, Opus, and WAV, with configurable quality and WAV corruption resilience.

## What this covers

- `AudioRecorder` abstraction with `AacRecorder` and `WavRecorder` implementations; `AudioFormat` model (AAC / Auto / Opus / WAV).
- Recording-quality knobs: Voice/Hi-Fi mode, bitrate (64–320 kbps), sample rate — via `AudioConfigHandler` and `FormatHandler`.
- WAV corruption resilience via `WavHeaderRecovery`.

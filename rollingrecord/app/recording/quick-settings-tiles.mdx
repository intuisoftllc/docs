Quick Settings tiles let users start, stop, and protect recordings straight from the notification shade.

## What this covers

- `RecordingTileService` (start/stop from the shade) and `ProtectedModeTileService` (protect a rolling segment).
- `TileStartActivity` launch path, `TileRefresher` state updates, and `TileHaptics`; the `TileMode` setting (Manual / Rolling new / Rolling pinned session).

A small admission layer decides when a recording may start and prevents microphone and playback contention.

## What this covers

- `RecordingGatekeeper`, `RecordingAdmissionBus`, `RecordStartRequest`, and `BlockingOp` for admitting/queuing start requests.
- `BusyGuard` mutual exclusion and `PlaybackCoordinator` to avoid mic/playback contention.

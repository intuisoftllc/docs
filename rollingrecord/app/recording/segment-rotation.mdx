Long and rolling captures are split into fixed-duration segments that roll over as recording continues.

## What this covers

- `SegmentRotationManager` + `SegmentRotationHandler` split long/rolling captures into segments (1 / 5 / 15 / 30 min).
- How segments relate to a `RecordingSession` and appear in Session Detail.

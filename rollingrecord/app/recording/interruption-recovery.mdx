Recording survives interruptions gracefully — pausing on calls, auto-recovering from audio-focus loss, and reporting recovery after a kill.

## What this covers

- `CallStateHandler` pauses on phone calls (manual resume); audio-focus loss and mic contention auto-recover rather than pausing.
- `RecordingInterruptedState` + `V2RecordingInterruptedSheet` surfacing the outcome to the user.
- The "Block calls while recording" setting.

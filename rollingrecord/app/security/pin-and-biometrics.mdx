App Lock unlocks with a PIN or biometrics, with lockout backoff and a synced PIN verifier.

## What this covers

- `AppLockCrypto` and `AppLockVerifier` (crypto module) PIN verification; `PinLockoutPolicy` backoff.
- `AppLockBiometricGate` / `BiometricHelper`; `V2PinEntryScreen` / `V2PinKeypad` UI.
- Remote PIN registration: `SetUserPinApi` / `SetUserPinApiClient`.
